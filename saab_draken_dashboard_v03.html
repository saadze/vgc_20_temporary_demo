External stochastic environment

For each simulated future, events are sampled from adjustable ranges and annual event probabilities.

Interpretation: These are illustrative probability and severity ranges, not estimates of actual aviation-market hazards. A demand shock temporarily depresses sales; competitor entry reduces achievable share in this demonstration model.

Define minimum success thresholds

A future fails if it breaches any selected requirement. Change these criteria to explore how robust strategy rankings change.
Cost cap vs capital available: the success cost cap is a user-defined criterion; available capital separately determines whether a strategy is feasible without additional funding. Both conditions are checked in the demonstration.

How decision-makers could use signposts

Monitor

Track demand indicators, forecast revisions, supplier/technology delays and capacity changes.

Reassess

When signposts are crossed, re-run all strategies under updated uncertainty distributions.

Adapt

Explore staged funding, partnering, redirecting or terminating under an eventual optimized decision policy.

Model integrity: All stochastic rates, costs, contribution margins, market shares and correlations in this browser demo are invented for interface exploration. The output bands represent distributions across simulated futures, not estimation confidence intervals.
